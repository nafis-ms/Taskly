Features

Everything you need to stay on track

Taskly brings your to-dos, reminders and daily schedule into one calm place, so you always know what to do next and never miss a deadline.

To-Do lists

Capture every task in seconds, tick it off when it is done and keep your day uncluttered. Finished work moves to History automatically.

Task reminders

Set a deadline and choose how early to be reminded, in days, hours or minutes. Taskly emails you at that moment, with an in-app notification as well.

Routine & daily plans

Build a repeating weekly routine, or plan a single day or date. Sort items into categories such as Study, Work, Fitness, Meal and Appointment.

24-hour dashboard

See today at a glance: your greeting, the day’s schedule, the next upcoming reminder, a 24-hour cycle and the live time, all in one place.

History

Look back at what you completed and what you planned. A clear record helps you spot patterns and build discipline over time.

Synced, with support built in

Your data is saved to your account, so it follows you across devices. Light and dark themes, and live chat with the Taskly team, are one tap away.

About us

Built to help you do what you planned

Taskly started with a simple frustration: tasks in one app, reminders in another and a schedule somewhere else. We built a single, friendly place for all three, a To-Do list, reminders and a daily schedule that work together, to help you stay organised and disciplined without the stress.

Discipline made simple

Good habits come from small, repeated actions. Taskly keeps your tasks, routine and reminders together so following through becomes the easy choice.

Calm by design

No clutter and no noise. Every screen shows only what matters right now, and the app stays out of your way until you need it.

Built with our community

Feedback from real users shapes what we build next. Share your thoughts below or chat with us live, and we read every message.
